## Output rights

Repository owner confirmed on 2026-07-27 that tracked model/provider outputs may be redistributed. Those
outputs are offered under CC BY 4.0 only to the extent the owner holds licensable rights. Underlying model,
runtime, and provider terms continue to apply.

Generated local audit, run, and evidence output directories are ignored and are not part of the tracked
dataset. New contributions must document source, collection method, permission or license, privacy review,
generation method, model/provider/runtime identity when applicable, and any transformation from source.
