## Route Sections

### BACKGROUND
NIST developed the AI Risk Management Framework to help organisations manage risks to individuals, organisations, and society from AI systems. It is intended as a voluntary framework that can be used across sectors and technologies.

### DEFINE
AI risk management is the structured process of identifying, measuring, prioritising, and responding to potential harms that may arise from AI design, development, deployment, or use.

### CLAIM
AI risk is not only a model property. It also depends on data, people, deployment setting, organisational process, affected communities, and monitoring over time.

### METHOD
The framework can be operationalised by mapping the AI system context, measuring risks and impacts, managing mitigations, and governing the process with accountability structures.

### LIMITATION
Risk measurement for AI systems remains difficult because harms can be contextual, delayed, distributed across actors, or difficult to quantify before deployment.
