## Route Sections

### BACKGROUND
The EU AI Act creates a regulatory framework for AI systems, including lifecycle obligations for high-risk AI systems.

### DEFINE
A risk management system for high-risk AI is a continuous process for identifying, analysing, evaluating, mitigating, and reviewing risks across the system lifecycle.

### CLAIM
Risk management is not a one-time document; it must be maintained and updated as the AI system and its deployment conditions change.

### METHOD
A provider should identify known and foreseeable risks, estimate and evaluate those risks, implement mitigation measures, and test whether the controls are effective.

### LIMITATION
Compliance with a risk management process does not mean all risks are eliminated; residual risk and context of use still matter.

### EXAMPLE
A model used in a high-risk setting may require special attention to vulnerable groups and foreseeable misuse.
