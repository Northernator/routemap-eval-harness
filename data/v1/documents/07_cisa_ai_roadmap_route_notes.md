## Route Sections

### BACKGROUND
CISA's AI roadmap describes how the agency approaches AI in relation to cybersecurity, critical infrastructure, and national risk management.

### DEFINE
An AI roadmap is a strategic plan that links organisational mission, capabilities, risks, governance, and operational priorities.

### CLAIM
AI can improve cyber defence, but it can also expand adversary capabilities, introduce new dependencies, and require new forms of assurance.

### METHOD
A roadmap-style workflow should identify mission use cases, evaluate risk, coordinate stakeholders, build internal capability, and support secure adoption.

### LIMITATION
A roadmap sets direction, but it does not by itself validate individual systems or resolve all operational tradeoffs.

### RESULT
This document is useful because it contains strategic CLAIMs, METHOD-like actions, and LIMITATION passages about adoption constraints.

### NEXT_STEP
RouteMap should test whether strategic goals can be separated from concrete methods and risk limitations.

### EXAMPLE
A passage about using AI to improve cyber operations should be treated differently from a passage warning about adversarial AI misuse.
