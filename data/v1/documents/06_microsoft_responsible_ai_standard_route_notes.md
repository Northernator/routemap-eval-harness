## Route Sections

### BACKGROUND
Microsoft describes responsible AI as a set of principles and practices for designing, building, testing, and deploying AI systems responsibly.

### DEFINE
A responsible AI standard converts broad principles into product-development requirements, governance checkpoints, and review practices.

### CLAIM
Principles alone are not sufficient unless they are connected to concrete engineering, review, and accountability processes.

### METHOD
A responsible AI workflow can include impact assessment, risk review, human oversight, testing, documentation, release controls, and monitoring.

### RESULT
This document should produce RouteMap labels where principle statements are CLAIMs and product-development requirements are METHODs.

### LIMITATION
A company standard can improve consistency, but external validation and context-specific risk analysis are still needed.

### EXAMPLE
A fairness review should be labelled METHOD when it describes an assessment step, but LIMITATION when it describes unresolved fairness uncertainty.

### NEXT_STEP
RouteMap should test whether it can distinguish aspirational principle language from enforceable process language.
