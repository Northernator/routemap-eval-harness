## Route Sections

### BACKGROUND
The NCSC guidance treats AI security as a lifecycle problem rather than a one-time deployment checklist.

### DEFINE
Secure AI system development means designing, building, deploying, operating, and maintaining AI systems with explicit security responsibilities and mitigations at each stage.

### METHOD
The lifecycle can be routed through secure design, secure development, secure deployment, and secure operation and maintenance.

### CLAIM
Security outcomes cannot be delegated entirely to end users; organisations building AI systems need to take responsibility for foreseeable misuse, monitoring, updates, and transparency.

### EXAMPLE
Secure operation includes logging, monitoring, update management, incident response planning, and information sharing after deployment.

### LIMITATION
The guidance gives general mitigations but does not by itself prove that a specific model or deployment is safe; implementation evidence is still required.

### NEXT_STEP
RouteMap should test whether scattered passages about design, deployment, and maintenance are linked as a single lifecycle chain.

### RESULT
This document is suitable for dependency-chain retrieval because the relevant guidance is distributed across multiple lifecycle phases.
