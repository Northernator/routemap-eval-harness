## Route Sections

### BACKGROUND
The ICO guidance explains how data protection principles apply when organisations build or use AI systems involving personal data.

### DEFINE
Personal data in AI includes information relating to an identified or identifiable person, including data used in training, testing, deployment, profiling, or explanation.

### CLAIM
AI systems can create data protection risks because they may process large datasets, infer sensitive attributes, automate decisions, or make explanations difficult.

### METHOD
A compliant AI workflow should consider lawful basis, fairness, transparency, data minimisation, security, accountability, and data protection impact assessment where appropriate.

### LIMITATION
Technical accuracy alone does not settle whether an AI system is fair, lawful, or transparent under data protection law.

### EXAMPLE
A model that predicts user behaviour from behavioural data may require a privacy assessment even if it does not directly reveal the training data.
