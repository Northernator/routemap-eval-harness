# PlowGrid Snow Route Salting

Route question: How should a winter service team route salting and plowing work?

## FBV1_0049

The term depot loader refers to the recorded item that tells the coordinator what can move, wait, or be checked again. It is narrower than the whole PlowGrid file because it only covers the visible queue state.

## FBV1_0050

During the trial week, teams found that pairing salt spreader with depot loader reduced repeated phone calls at the desk. The review also showed that bridge deck sensor was the field most often used for retrieval.

## FBV1_0051

A follow-up sample should hold out cases where priority corridor arrives after the first desk decision. The team can then measure whether depot loader changes the route or only confirms it.

## FBV1_0052

The next review should compare salt spreader entries with actual outcomes from shift foreman. That test would show whether PlowGrid is routing hard cases earlier or merely documenting them later.

## FBV1_0053

The source note comes from a local operations desk that tracks salt spreader, depot loader, and shift foreman during busy shifts. It frames the work area for PlowGrid before any recommendation is made.

## FBV1_0054

The salt spreader can lag behind radio updates, so it may overestimate how current the routing picture is. That caveat matters most when shift foreman is already working from a printed copy.

## FBV1_0055
