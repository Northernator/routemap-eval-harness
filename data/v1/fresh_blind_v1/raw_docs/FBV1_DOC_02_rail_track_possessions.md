# TrackWindow Rail Possession Planning

Route question: How should a rail maintenance office route track possession requests?

## FBV1_0017

The TrackWindow desk is not sufficient when ballast train is missing from the handoff, even if possession calendar looks complete. A coordinator still needs the missing evidence before the route can be trusted.

## FBV1_0018

During the trial week, teams found that pairing possession calendar with ballast train reduced repeated phone calls at the desk. The review also showed that night crew was the field most often used for retrieval.

## FBV1_0019

Operators first sort the ballast train by urgency, then attach the matching night crew entry to each open case. The final pass sends exceptions back to the supervisor queue rather than closing them silently.

## FBV1_0020

The routing decision is more reliable when possession calendar and signal isolation plan are reviewed together instead of in separate calls. That pairing keeps night crew from being treated as a side note after the main choice is already made.
