# Annotation Quick Reference

## Roles

- DEFINE: establishes the meaning of a term, variable, clause, or concept.
- CLAIM: asserts something that may need evidence or comparison.
- METHOD: explains how something is computed, tested, implemented, or performed.
- RESULT: reports an observed outcome, measurement, finding, or conclusion.
- LIMITATION: states a caveat, boundary, weakness, uncertainty, or excluded validity.
- NEXT_STEP: proposes future work, follow-up testing, or planned action.
- EXAMPLE: illustrates without changing the operative rule or main claim.
- BACKGROUND: supplies context, history, or framing without doing the main work.
- MODIFY: changes, widens, narrows, updates, or qualifies an existing rule or definition.
- EXCEPT: carves out a case from an otherwise applicable rule.
- SUPPORTS: gives evidence for another claim.
- CONTRADICTS: gives evidence against another claim.
- DEPENDS_ON: states a dependency or precondition.

## Operative Status

- OPERATIVE: active, binding, or structurally used in the current document.
- BACKGROUND: contextual but not active as a rule, method, or result.
- EXAMPLE: illustrative only.
- REJECTED_DRAFT: considered but explicitly rejected or superseded.
- HISTORICAL: true of past versions, prior drafts, or historical context.
- NON_BINDING: advisory, illustrative, or not enforceable.
- UNKNOWN: unclear from the passage.

## Relations

- defines: gives meaning to an entity or term.
- modifies: changes an existing definition, rule, method, or claim.
- excepts: removes a specific case from an otherwise applicable rule.
- supports: provides evidence or justification.
- contradicts: provides counterevidence or conflict.
- depends_on: requires another condition, system, rule, or premise.
- causes: describes causal effect.
- measures: describes measurement or computation.
- reports: states an observed result or finding.
- limits: describes a caveat, weakness, or boundary.
- next_step: points to follow-up work.
- background_to: provides context for another passage or topic.

## Common Confusions

### MODIFY vs CLAIM

Use MODIFY when the passage changes an existing rule, meaning, scope, or method. Use CLAIM when it asserts something without changing an earlier rule.

### EXCEPT vs LIMITATION

Use EXCEPT when a specific case is carved out of a rule. Use LIMITATION when the passage describes a general caveat, weakness, uncertainty, or scope boundary.

### BACKGROUND vs EXAMPLE

Use BACKGROUND for context or history. Use EXAMPLE for an illustration that demonstrates a concept without being operative.

### CLAIM vs RESULT

Use CLAIM for an assertion that may need support. Use RESULT when the passage reports an actual observed outcome, experiment result, or evaluation finding.

### METHOD vs NEXT_STEP

Use METHOD for how work is done now. Use NEXT_STEP for work planned later.
