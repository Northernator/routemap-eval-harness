# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free project for contributors and users regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, limitations, and conflicting results.
- Respect privacy, attribution, dataset rights, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior promptly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, publication of private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-run events, and public
representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For non-sensitive concerns, flag the relevant content to maintainers. For a sensitive report, use the
repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and identify
the submission as a conduct report. If that channel is unavailable, open a minimal issue asking for private
maintainer follow-up without including report details.

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical, investigate in good faith, and avoid conflicts
of interest. Responses may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent
exclusion, depending on impact and pattern of behavior. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
